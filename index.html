Tournament rules

Group stage

  • Seven groups of six players.
  • Each player plays every other player in their group exactly once: five matches per player.
  • Matches are best of five games; each game is played to 11 points.
  • The published date and time are the default appointment. Players may change them by mutual agreement.
  • Players should make every reasonable effort to complete all five group matches.

Ranking

  1. Match wins
  2. Win rate
  3. Set ratio
  4. Point differential

Eligibility & qualification

  • A player must complete at least three group-stage matches to be eligible for the knockout stage.
  • The top two eligible players in each group qualify automatically: 14 players.
  • The two best eligible third-place finishers across the seven groups also qualify.
  • The knockout stage therefore begins with 16 players.

Forfeits

A forfeit counts as a match win/loss and as a match played, but no artificial sets or points are added.

Unresolved exact tie

If players remain exactly tied after all four ranking criteria, the organizer will apply a final tiebreak decision. This edge case has intentionally not been hard-coded yet.
